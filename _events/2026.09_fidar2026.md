We are pleased to announce the first edition of the Summer School "FiDAR2026: Digital Philology and Network Analysis", organized collaboratively by the universities of Zaragoza, Verona, and Córdoba, with the participation of Freie Universität Berlin.

The Summer School aims to provide training and consolidate expertise in two of the most established areas of the Digital Humanities: the creation of editions based on the principles of digital philology, and data processing using network analysis tools.

The initiative seeks to train early-career scholars, fostering digital literacy and the independent development of research projects through the experience and expertise of established research teams: COMEDIC, Progetto Mambrino, PARAGLIPA, and CalDracor. The School is also part of the activities supported by Spain’s Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities, and has received the support of the Asociación de Humanidades Digitales Hispánicas (HDH) and the DAIH (Digital Arena for Inclusive Humanities) Centre at the University of Verona.
